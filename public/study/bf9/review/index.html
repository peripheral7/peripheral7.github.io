9.1Relevant cash flow

Definition — the change in the firm's total future cash flow that is a direct consequence of accepting the project.

With and without — not "before and after"

  • Firm cash flow with the project minus without it = incremental cash flow
  • If an existing division's sales fall after launch, the part that would have fallen anyway is not the project's fault

Stand-alone principle

  • Once incremental cash flows are found, evaluate the project as a mini-firm — with its own sales, costs, assets, and cash flows
  • No need to value the whole firm twice

9.1관련 현금흐름 (relevant cash flow)

정의 — 프로젝트를 채택하기로 한 결정의 직접적 결과로 생기는 회사 전체 미래 현금흐름의 변화.

With and without — "전후"가 아니다

  • 프로젝트가 있을 때와 없을 때의 회사 현금흐름 차이 = 증분 현금흐름 (incremental cash flow)
  • 출시 뒤 기존 사업부 매출이 줄어도, 어차피 줄었을 몫은 프로젝트 탓이 아니다

독립 원칙 (stand-alone principle)

  • 증분 현금흐름을 찾고 나면 프로젝트를 작은 회사 하나처럼 — 자체 매출·비용·자산·현금흐름으로 — 평가한다
  • 회사 전체를 두 번 평가할 필요가 없다

9.2Incremental call — "Does this cash flow occur even if we reject the project?"

Yes → not incremental, exclude.  No → incremental, include (regardless of accounting treatment).

ItemCallRule · example
Sunk costOutAlready paid or irrevocably committed. E.g., completed marketing study $120,000. Exclude even if capitalized and expensed later
Opportunity costInBest alternative use of an owned asset. Charge today's market value (net of sale taxes) — not purchase price or book value
Erosion (cannibalization)In (−)Cash flow taken from existing products. Exclude the part competitors would take anyway
SynergyIn (+)Boost to existing product sales
Net working capital (NWC)InOutflow at start, recovered at end. Nominal sum is zero, yet a real cost via time value
Financing costs (interest, dividends, principal)OutAlready in the discount rate (required return, Ch.12). Deducting = double-counting → no interest line in pro forma

9.2증분 판단 — "프로젝트를 기각해도 이 현금흐름이 생기는가?"

생긴다 → 증분 아님, 제외.  생기지 않는다 → 증분, 포함 (회계 처리와 무관).

9.3Pro forma → OCF → project cash flow

Pro forma income statement

SalesUnits × price
− Variable costsUnits × cost/unit
− Fixed costsIncremental only
− DepreciationTax depreciation
= EBIT
− TaxesEBIT × T
= NOPATEBIT × (1 − T)

No interest line → NOPAT = net income. Depreciation enters only because it lowers taxes.

Project cash flow — just three pieces

Project CF = OCF − Net capital spending − ΔNWC

OCF = EBIT + D − Taxes = NOPAT + D

  • Net capital spending: asset purchase (Year 0); after-tax sale proceeds at the end are an inflow
  • ΔNWC: increase = outflow; unless told otherwise, fully recovered in the last year
  • Shape: big outflow at Year 0 → middle years are OCF only → last year is large from NWC and salvage recovery

9.3Pro forma → OCF → 프로젝트 현금흐름

추정 손익계산서 (pro forma)

SalesUnits × price
− Variable costsUnits × cost/unit
− Fixed costs증분만
− Depreciation세법 감가상각
= EBIT
− TaxesEBIT × T
= NOPATEBIT × (1 − T)

이자 줄 없음 → NOPAT = 순이익(net income). 감가상각은 세금을 줄이기 때문에만 들어간다.

프로젝트 현금흐름 — 세 요소뿐

Project CF = OCF − 순자본지출 − ΔNWC

OCF = EBIT + D − Taxes = NOPAT + D

  • 순자본지출 (capital spending): 자산 구입(Year 0), 끝에 세후 매각대금은 유입
  • ΔNWC: 증가 = 유출, 별말 없으면 마지막 해에 전액 회수
  • 모양: Year 0 큰 유출 → 중간 연도는 OCF뿐 → 마지막 해 NWC·잔존가치 회수로 큼

9.4Net working capital (NWC) — sales are not cash receipts

Accrual vs cash

  • Accounting records sales and costs when they occur (accrual basis). Credit sale of $1,000 → sales $1,000, receivables $1,000, cash 0
  • Cash collected = sales − increase in receivables
  • Pro forma is accrual → subtracting ΔNWC converts back to cash (it is not an extra cost)

Sign and timing

SituationCash flow
NWC increases (inventory, receivables up)Outflow (−)
NWC decreases (receivables collected)Inflow (+)
Last yearRemaining NWC fully recovered (+)

In growth, NWC rises with sales so early adjustments are negative (−); it reverses when the business winds down. Count the change (Δ), not the level.

9.4순운전자본 (NWC) — 매출과 현금 수입은 다르다

발생주의 vs 현금

  • 회계는 거래 시점에 매출·비용을 기록 (accrual basis). 외상 매출 $1,000 → 매출 $1,000, 매출채권 $1,000, 현금 0
  • 현금 회수 = 매출 − 매출채권 증가
  • pro forma는 발생주의 → ΔNWC를 빼는 것이 현금으로 되돌리는 보정이다 (추가 비용이 아님)

부호와 시점

상황현금흐름
NWC 증가 (재고·채권 늘어남)유출 (−)
NWC 감소 (채권 회수)유입 (+)
마지막 해남은 NWC 전액 회수 (+)

성장기에는 NWC가 매출과 함께 늘어 초반 조정이 음(−), 사업을 접을 때 반대로 돈다. 셀 것은 수준이 아니라 변화(Δ).

9.4Tax depreciation — straight-line vs MACRS

  • Straight-line: cost ÷ useful life, down to zero or a given salvage value. Course default
  • MACRS: each asset has a class life; fixed percentage of original cost each year
  • Half-year convention: assumes mid-year purchase → 3-year class depreciates over 4 years, 5-year class over 6
  • MACRS ignores salvage value, always depreciates to zero
  • Total deductions are equal — MACRS only accelerates them

9.4세법 감가상각 — 정액법 vs MACRS

  • 정액법 (straight-line): 원가 ÷ 내용연수, 0 또는 주어진 잔존가치까지. 수업 기본값
  • MACRS: 자산마다 class life, 매년 원가(original cost)에 고정 비율

  • MACRS는 잔존가치를 무시, 항상 0까지 상각
  • 총 공제액은 같다 — MACRS는 공제를 앞당길 뿐

9.4Book value vs market value — after-tax salvage

After-tax salvage = MV − T × (MV − BV)

Sold above book value (MV > BV)

  • Too much depreciation was taken → gain is taxed (recapture)
  • After-tax proceeds < sale price

Sold below book value (MV < BV)

  • Too little depreciation was taken → loss deduction, add the tax savings
  • After-tax proceeds > sale price

Lecture example: $90,000, straight-line 5 years, sold after 3 → BV = 90,000 − 3 × 18,000 = 36,000. Sell at $25,000: loss 11,000 × 0.21 = 2,310 tax saved → $27,310; sell at $45,000: gain 9,000 × 0.21 = 1,890 taxed → $43,110.

BV = cost − accumulated depreciation. Selling a MACRS asset before its class life ends leaves the remaining percentages as BV.

9.4장부가 vs 시장가 — 세후 잔존가치 (after-tax salvage)

After-tax salvage = MV − T × (MV − BV)

장부가보다 비싸게 판매 (MV > BV)

  • 감가상각을 너무 많이 받았다 → 차익 환수 과세 (recapture)
  • 세후 대금 < 매각가

장부가보다 싸게 판매 (MV < BV)

  • 감가상각을 덜 받았다 → 손실 공제, 절세액을 더함
  • 세후 대금 > 매각가

강의 예: $90,000, 정액 5년, 3년 뒤 매각 → BV = 90,000 − 3 × 18,000 = 36,000. $25,000에 팔면 손실 11,000 × 0.21 = 2,310 절세 → $27,310; $45,000에 팔면 차익 9,000 × 0.21 = 1,890 과세 → $43,110.

BV = 원가 − 누적 감가상각. MACRS 자산을 class life 전에 팔면 남은 비율만큼 BV가 남는다.

9.5Three OCF definitions — one identity rearranged

MethodFormulaLecture project (S 160,000 · C 110,000 · D 30,000 · T 21%)
Bottom-upOCF = NOPAT + D
(textbook: net income + D, same when no interest)		15,800 + 30,000 = 45,800

Tax shieldOCF = (Sales − Costs)(1 − T) + D × T39,500 + 6,300 = 45,800
  • Depreciation tax shield = D × T — the only way depreciation affects cash flow
  • D is not visible in the top-down formula, but it is inside Taxes
  • If the three differ, there is a calculation error — use them to cross-check each other

Solution steps · Common mistakes

Solution steps

  1. In / Out call for each item (exclude sunk costs and interest; include opportunity cost and erosion)
  2. Depreciation schedule (straight-line or MACRS) → BV at sale
  3. Pro forma: EBIT → Taxes → NOPAT
  4. OCF = NOPAT + D (cross-check with another definition)
  5. Year 0: −asset − NWC; last year: + NWC + after-tax salvage
  6. NPV · IRR (BA II Plus CF worksheet)

Common mistakes (Sorensen example, correct NPV $11,565.74)

MistakeWrong NPV
Include sunk cost $75,000−63,434.26
Omit NWC recovery−59,483.89
Subtract after-tax interest−36,772.75
Omit opportunity cost (rent)+103,639.05

The first three lead to rejecting a good project; the last one badly inflates value.

풀이 순서 · 자주 하는 실수

풀이 순서

  1. 항목별 In / Out 판단 (매몰·이자 제외, 기회비용·잠식 포함)
  2. 감가상각표 (정액 또는 MACRS) → 매각 시 BV
  3. Pro forma: EBIT → Taxes → NOPAT
  4. OCF = NOPAT + D (다른 정의로 검산)
  5. Year 0: −자산 − NWC; 마지막 해: + NWC + 세후 잔존가치
  6. NPV · IRR (BA II Plus CF 워크시트)

Q1With and without

A division's sales are currently $2,000,000. Because of a competitor's entry, next year's sales were already expected to fall 5%. After the firm launched a new product, the division's sales fell 8%. How much of the decline is due to the new product (erosion)? Also explain why a "before and after" launch comparison is wrong.

Without (no project): −5% → 2,000,000 × 5% = 100,000 decline — due to the competitor, happens anyway

With (project): −8% → 2,000,000 × 8% = 160,000 decline

Incremental erosion = (8% − 5%) × 2,000,000 = $60,000 (in the analysis, subtract the contribution margin lost on these sales as a cost)

Before/after blames the full 160,000 on the new product and undervalues the project. The benchmark is "had we not launched," not "before launch."

Q1With and without

한 사업부의 매출은 현재 $2,000,000이다. 경쟁사 진입 때문에 내년 매출이 5% 줄 것으로 이미 예상돼 있었다. 회사가 신제품을 출시한 뒤 이 사업부 매출은 8% 줄었다. 매출 감소 중 신제품 때문인 부분(잠식, erosion)은 얼마인가? 출시 "전후(before and after)" 비교가 왜 틀리는지도 설명하라.

Without (프로젝트 없음): −5% → 2,000,000 × 5% = 100,000 감소 — 경쟁사 탓, 어차피 발생

With (프로젝트 있음): −8% → 2,000,000 × 8% = 160,000 감소

증분 잠식 = (8% − 5%) × 2,000,000 = $60,000 (분석에서는 이 매출에서 잃는 공헌이익을 비용으로 뺀다)

전후 비교는 160,000 전부를 신제품 탓으로 돌려 프로젝트를 과소평가한다. 기준은 "출시 전"이 아니라 "출시하지 않았을 때"다.

Q2Sunk cost

The firm has already spent $3.0 million on a prototype (non-refundable). Finishing costs $1.2 million more, and the PV of the finished product's future cash flows is $2.0 million. An executive says, "Total cost 4.2 > value 2.0, so stop." Is this right?

Today's decision: finishing gives +2.0 − 1.2 = NPV +$0.8 million > 0 → finish

The $3.0 million is gone whether we finish or stop — no decision can change it, so drop it from the analysis entirely

Stopping on the executive's logic throws away 0.8 million of value — wasting the sunk cost "twice" (lecture p.7)

Note: spending not yet paid but irrevocably committed is also sunk — even if capitalized and expensed later.

Q2매몰원가 (sunk cost)

회사는 시제품 개발에 이미 $3.0 million을 썼다(환불 불가). 완성하려면 $1.2 million이 더 들고, 완성된 제품의 미래 현금흐름 현재가치는 $2.0 million이다. 한 임원은 "총비용 4.2 > 가치 2.0이니 중단하자"고 한다. 옳은가?

오늘의 결정: 완성하면 +2.0 − 1.2 = NPV +$0.8 million > 0 → 완성한다

$3.0 million은 완성하든 중단하든 이미 사라졌다 — 어떤 결정도 바꿀 수 없으므로 분석에서 완전히 뺀다

임원의 논리대로 중단하면 0.8 million의 가치를 버린다 — 매몰원가를 "두 번" 낭비하는 셈 (강의 p.7)

주의: 아직 돈이 나가지 않았어도 취소할 수 없게 약정된(irrevocably committed) 지출은 매몰원가다. 자본화돼 나중에 비용 처리되더라도 마찬가지.

Q3Opportunity cost

A project uses a warehouse the firm bought years ago for $200,000 (book value also $200,000). It could be sold today for $550,000. Tax rate 21%. What opportunity cost is charged to the project at Year 0? How is the purchase price treated?

Rule: today's market value, net of tax on the sale (lecture p.8 "net of any tax on the sale")

Tax on gain = 0.21 × (550,000 − 200,000) = 73,500

Opportunity cost = 550,000 − 73,500 = $476,500 → −476,500 in the Year 0 cash flow

The $200,000 purchase price is sunk — not used in the decision. Book value is not an economic cost either (used only for tax calculation).

"We already own it, so it's free" — the most common mistake that makes bad projects look good.

Q3기회비용 (opportunity cost)

프로젝트가 회사가 몇 년 전 $200,000에 산 창고를 쓴다(장부가도 $200,000). 이 창고는 오늘 $550,000에 팔 수 있다. 세율 21%. Year 0에 프로젝트에 부과할 기회비용은? 매입가는 어떻게 처리하나?

규칙: 오늘 시장가치, 매각 세금 차감 후 (강의 p.8 "net of any tax on the sale")

매각 차익 세금 = 0.21 × (550,000 − 200,000) = 73,500

기회비용 = 550,000 − 73,500 = $476,500 → Year 0 현금흐름에 −476,500

매입가 $200,000은 매몰원가 — 판단에 쓰지 않는다. 장부가도 경제적 비용이 아니다(세금 계산에만 쓰인다).

"이미 갖고 있으니 공짜" — 나쁜 프로젝트를 좋아 보이게 만드는 가장 흔한 실수.

Q4Side effects: erosion and synergy

The new product also boosts accessory sales, adding $25,000 of contribution per year. What is the project's annual incremental contribution?

Erosion — only the part the project causes: 100,000 − 40,000 = 60,000

Synergy: +25,000

Q6Financing costs stay out of cash flow

A project has EBIT of $180,000 per year and depreciation of $50,000 per year. Part of the funding is a loan, with interest of $30,000 per year. Tax rate 21%. What taxes and OCF go into the project cash flow forecast?

No interest line in the pro forma → tax directly on EBIT: Taxes = 0.21 × 180,000 = $37,800

OCF = EBIT + D − Taxes = 180,000 + 50,000 − 37,800 = $192,200

Wrong answer 0.21 × (180,000 − 30,000) = 31,500: puts the interest tax shield into cash flow — that effect belongs in the discount rate (Ch.12)

Deducting interest and also discounting at a rate that includes the cost of debt penalizes the same borrowing twice. So project taxes are "the taxes we would pay if all-equity financed."

Q6금융비용은 현금흐름에서 뺀다

프로젝트의 EBIT는 연 $180,000, 감가상각은 연 $50,000이다. 자금 일부를 대출로 조달해 이자가 연 $30,000이다. 세율 21%. 프로젝트 현금흐름 예측에 넣을 세금과 OCF는?

OCF = EBIT + D − Taxes = 180,000 + 50,000 − 37,800 = $192,200

오답 0.21 × (180,000 − 30,000) = 31,500: 이자 절세효과를 현금흐름에 넣은 것 — 그 효과는 할인율(Ch.12) 쪽에 있다

이자를 빼고 부채비용이 들어간 할인율로 다시 할인하면 같은 차입을 두 번 벌주는 셈. 그래서 프로젝트 세금은 "전액 자기자본 조달일 때 낼 세금"이다.

Q15After-tax salvage — loss case and gain case

An asset costing $90,000 is depreciated straight-line over 5 years, but the project ends after 3 years. Tax rate 21%. What is the after-tax salvage value if sold (a) for $25,000 (b) for $45,000?

Annual depreciation = 90,000 / 5 = 18,000 → BV = 90,000 − 3 × 18,000 = 36,000

After-tax salvage = MV − T × (MV − BV)

(a) MV 25,000 < BV

Loss 11,000 → tax saved 0.21 × 11,000 = 2,310

= 25,000 − 0.21 × (25,000 − 36,000) = $27,310 (more than the sale price)

(b) MV 45,000 > BV

Gain 9,000 → tax 0.21 × 9,000 = 1,890

= 45,000 − 0.21 × (45,000 − 36,000) = $43,110 (less than the sale price)

Q17Three OCF definitions, same answer
