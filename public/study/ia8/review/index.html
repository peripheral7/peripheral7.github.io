8.1 RANDOM WALKS AND EFFICIENT MARKETS

Forecasts destroy themselves — the random walk

The logic (once a forecast is public)

  1. A "+20% in 3 days" signal appears
  2. Everyone who sees it buys today (no reason to wait)
  3. Price jumps ≈ +20% now → no longer cheap
  4. The predictable part is gone; only the unforecastable movement remains

All forecastable information is already in the price. If it were not, someone would trade, and that trading puts the information into the price.

E(Pt+1) = Pt × (1 + E(r))

A random walk is: successive price changes that are independent and random. New information is unpredictable by definition → so is the price change after news.

A random walk is not: prices moving arbitrarily ✕ · zero return ✕ (it drifts at the expected return) · and a chart that looks like a random walk does not prove efficiency.

Example: Pt = $50, E(r) = 1% → $50.50. Yesterday's move is already in the price.

E(Pt+1) = Pt × (1 + E(r))

random walk 다: 연속된 가격 변화는 서로 독립적이고 무작위. 새 정보는 정의상 예측 불가 → 뉴스 뒤의 가격 변화도 예측 불가.

random walk가 아니다: 가격이 제멋대로라는 말 ✕ · 수익률이 0이라는 말 ✕ (기대수익률만큼 표류, drift) · 차트가 random walk처럼 보인다고 효율성이 증명되지도 않는다.

Three forms — only the scope of "all information" differs

Form (version)What prices already reflectWhat becomes useless
Weak formThe record of past prices and volumeTechnical analysis — charts, trends, momentum rules
Semistrong formAll public information: financial statements, forecasts, filings, news (+ past prices)Fundamental analysis based on public data
Strong formAll firm-related information, including what only insiders knowEverything — including inside information

Nesting: strong ⇒ semistrong ⇒ weak. Prices that reflect all public information also reflect past prices. The reverse does not hold.

The strong form is not seriously claimed: the very fact that insider trading must be banned is evidence that it works.

Classification tip: if the strategy uses ① only past prices and volume → violates weak ② public information → violates semistrong ③ nonpublic information → violates only strong

8.2 IMPLICATIONS OF THE EMH

Technical analysis and fundamental analysis

Technical analysis ↔ weak form

  • Assumes prices react slowly to information → you can ride trends (relative strength, resistance and support levels)
  • Past prices are free public information → competition erases patterns in them first
  • Self-destruction: "bounces at $70" → buy early at $72 → $75 … $70 never returns

The strongest objection, independent of evidence. Revisited with limits to arbitrage in Ch.9.

Fundamental analysis ↔ semistrong

  • Value a firm from earnings, dividends, prospects, and risk
  • Finding a good company is not enough — others read the same statements, and their conclusion is already in the price
  • The question to ask: "Is it a good company?" ✕ → "Is it better than the market already believes?" ○
  • Needs better data or interpretation consistently, after costs → an edge is plausible in neglected stocks and thinly traded markets

8.2 IMPLICATIONS OF THE EMH

기술적 분석과 기본적 분석

기술적 분석 (technical analysis) ↔ weak form

  • 가정: 가격이 정보에 천천히 반응 → 추세를 탈 수 있다 (relative strength, 저항선·지지선)
  • 과거 가격은 공짜 공개정보 → 그 안의 패턴은 경쟁이 가장 먼저 지운다
  • 자기파괴: "$70 에서 반등" → $72 에서 선매수 → $75 … $70 은 다시 오지 않는다

증거와 무관한 가장 강한 반론. Ch.9 에서 차익거래의 한계와 함께 다시 본다.

기본적 분석 (fundamental analysis) ↔ semistrong

  • 이익·배당·전망·위험으로 기업가치 평가
  • 좋은 기업을 찾는 것만으로는 부족 — 남들도 같은 재무제표를 읽었고, 그 결론은 이미 가격에 있다
  • 물어야 할 질문: "좋은 기업인가?" ✕ → "시장이 이미 믿는 것보다 나은가?" ○
  • 더 나은 자료나 해석이 꾸준히, 비용 차감 후에도 필요 → 우위는 소외 종목·참여자 적은 시장에서 그럴듯

8.2 IMPLICATIONS OF THE EMH

Active vs passive, and the portfolio management that remains

Passive: buy and hold a broad index · no research cost, low fees · accept the market return

Active: stock selection and market timing · needs excess return above its own costs

Net excess return = gross excess return − costs

Beat by 60 bp but charge 100 bp: 60 − 100 = −40 bp

Arithmetic constraint: wPrM + wAr̄A = rM → before costs r̄A = rM; after costs, below rM. Holds regardless of belief in efficiency.

What remains even in an efficient market

Risk and diversificationMatch risk tolerance; avoid bearing avoidable risk
TaxesHigh-income investor ≠ tax-exempt pension fund
Horizon and liquidity25-year-old saver ≠ retiree withdrawing
Hedging specific exposuresAn employee whose pay depends on one industry should not overweight it

Efficiency is a claim about prices, not about investors.

8.3 ARE MARKETS EFFICIENT?

Three testing hurdles and the joint-hypothesis problem

① Magnitude issue: $5bn × 0.1% = $5m — real performance, but buried in return noise and invisible statistically.

② Selection bias issue: a truly profitable rule is never published → studied strategies are those not good enough to keep secret.

③ Lucky event issue: with enough funds or rules, chance alone produces brilliant records. Example: 1,000 × 0.55 = 31.25 managers.

Better data cannot fix these — a reason the hypothesis is hard to refute, not a reason to believe it.

Joint-hypothesis problem

To call a return "abnormal," first define the normal return → an asset pricing model is required.

A rejection is ambiguous: either the market is inefficient or the model underestimates risk. The data cannot say which is wrong.

Partial escape = short-window event study: over a day or two, expected return is tiny relative to the price reaction, so the choice of model barely changes the result.

8.3 ARE MARKETS EFFICIENT?

검정의 세 장애물과 결합가설 문제

① 크기 (magnitude issue): $5bn × 0.1% = $5m — 진짜 성과지만 수익률 잡음(noise)에 묻혀 통계로 안 보인다.

② 선택편향 (selection bias issue): 정말 돈 버는 규칙은 공개하지 않는다 → 연구되는 전략은 비밀로 할 만큼 좋지 않았던 것들.

③ 운 (lucky event issue): 펀드·규칙이 충분히 많으면 우연만으로 뛰어난 기록이 나온다. 예: 1,000 × 0.55 = 31.25명.

더 좋은 데이터로 고칠 수 없다 — 가설을 반박하기 어려운 이유이지, 믿을 이유는 아니다.

결합가설 문제 (joint-hypothesis problem)

"비정상"이라 하려면 먼저 정상수익을 정해야 → 자산가격결정모형이 필요.

기각되면 모호: 시장이 비효율이거나 모형이 위험을 과소평가했거나. 데이터는 어느 쪽이 틀렸는지 말하지 못한다.

부분적 탈출구 = 단기 event study: 하루이틀 창에서는 기대수익이 가격 반응에 비해 아주 작아 모형 선택이 결과를 거의 바꾸지 않는다.

8.3 ARE MARKETS EFFICIENT?

Event study — abnormal return (AR) and cumulative (CAR)

et = rt − (a + b·rM,t)

CART = Σ et

CAPM benchmark: et = rt − [rf + β(rM,t − rf)]

  • a, b = intercept and slope (β) of the market model, estimated before the event
  • AR = return minus what the stock "would have earned anyway" given that day's market move → attributed to the event
  • CAR = running sum of AR → the path of the price reaction
  • Post-announcement drift = CARend − CARday 0
CAR shapeReading
Flat before · jump on the day · flat afterClean efficient reaction
Rises before the announcementInformation leak
Keeps moving the same way afterSlow reaction (underreaction)
Reverses after the announcementOverreaction

Both leakage and drift have been found in practice — which is why this method is the workhorse of the literature.

8.3 ARE MARKETS EFFICIENT?

Weak-form evidence · predicting aggregate market returns

The sign flips with the horizon

Short run (months)Long run (3–5 years)
Momentum: winners keep winning, losers keep losing → underreactionReversals: past losers beat past winners → overreaction

Both are results before transaction costs. Momentum needs frequent rebalancing, which eats a large share of the paper profit.

One explanation must produce both phenomena → why behavioral finance (Ch.9) is appealing

Predictability of market returns

High dividend yield or wide credit spreads tend to be followed by high market returns.

Inefficient reading: signals when the market is cheap.

Efficient reading: required return is high when the economy is bad and risk is hard to bear = time-varying risk premium.

Data cannot distinguish them — the most practical form of the joint-hypothesis problem

시장수익률 예측 가능성

배당수익률이 높거나 신용스프레드가 넓으면 이후 시장수익률이 높은 경향.

비효율 해석: 시장이 쌀 때를 알려준다.

효율 해석: 경기가 나빠 위험을 지기 어려울 때 요구수익률이 높다 = 시간가변 위험프리미엄 (time-varying risk premium).

데이터로는 구분 불가 — 결합가설 문제의 가장 실용적인 형태

Problem 1Which form is violated

If the following results are real after risk and cost adjustment, which form of the EMH (weak / semistrong / strong) do they violate? (a) A rule of buying stocks that hit a 52-week high earns consistent excess returns (b) Buying right after a share-buyback announcement and holding two weeks earns excess returns (c) An executive buys ahead of undisclosed clinical-trial results and earns excess returns (d) One fund beats the market in 7 of 10 years

(a) A rule using only past prices → violates the weak form. By nesting (strong ⇒ semistrong ⇒ weak), semistrong and strong fall too.
(b) An announcement is public information → violates semistrong (and so strong). It is not a past-price rule, so weak is not tested.
(c) Nonpublic information → violates only the strong form (the ban on insider trading is itself the evidence).
(d) Proves nothing — not risk-adjusted, and with many funds luck alone produces it (lucky event).
Key Classify by the type of information the strategy uses: past prices → weak, public information → semistrong, nonpublic → strong.

문제 1어떤 형태를 위배하나

다음 결과가 위험·비용 조정 후에도 진짜라면 EMH의 어떤 형태(weak / semistrong / strong)를 위배하나? (a) 52주 신고가를 경신한 종목을 사는 규칙이 꾸준히 초과수익 (b) 자사주 매입 공시 직후 사서 2주 보유하면 초과수익 (c) 임원이 미공개 임상시험 결과를 보고 미리 사서 초과수익 (d) 한 펀드가 10년 중 7년 시장을 이김

(a) 과거 가격만 쓰는 규칙 → weak form 위배. 포함관계(strong ⇒ semistrong ⇒ weak) 때문에 semistrong·strong 도 함께 무너진다.
(b) 공시는 공개정보 → semistrong 위배 (따라서 strong 도). 과거 가격 규칙이 아니므로 weak 는 검정하지 않는다.
(c) 미공개 정보 → strong form 만 위배 (내부자거래 금지 자체가 그 증거).
(d) 아무것도 증명 못 함 — 위험 조정 전이고, 펀드가 많으면 운만으로도 나온다(lucky event).
핵심 전략이 쓰는 정보의 종류로 분류한다: 과거 가격 → weak, 공개정보 → semistrong, 미공개 → strong.

Problem 2Random walk expected price

Hana Electronics stock is $120 today with a monthly expected return of 0.8%. Last week it rose 6%. (a) What is the expected price in one month? (b) In two months? (c) Evaluate the claim "it rose 6% last week, so it will ride the trend and rise further."

Formula E(Pt+1) = Pt × (1 + E(r)) — today's price already contains everything known
(a) 120 × 1.008 = $120.96
(b) 120 × 1.0082 = $121.93 · BA II Plus: 1.008 x² × 120 = 121.93
(c) Last week's rise is a past price = information already reflected in today's $120. It does not change the expected price (weak form). Price drifts at the expected return, not zero, and the rest is unpredictable noise.
Key Random walk ≠ zero return. It rises as expected, and only the movement beyond that is unpredictable.

문제 2Random walk 기대가격

하나전자 주가는 오늘 $120, 월 기대수익률은 0.8%. 지난주 이 주식은 6% 올랐다. (a) 한 달 뒤 기대가격은? (b) 두 달 뒤는? (c) "지난주 6% 올랐으니 추세를 타서 더 오를 것"이라는 주장을 평가하라.

공식 E(Pt+1) = Pt × (1 + E(r)) — 오늘 가격에 이미 알려진 모든 것이 들어 있다
(a) 120 × 1.008 = $120.96
(b) 120 × 1.0082 = $121.93 · BA II Plus: 1.008 x² × 120 = 121.93
(c) 지난주 상승은 과거 가격 = 이미 오늘의 $120 에 반영된 정보. 기대가격을 바꾸지 못한다(weak form). 가격은 0이 아니라 기대수익률만큼 표류(drift)하고, 나머지는 예측할 수 없는 noise.
핵심 random walk ≠ 수익률 0. 기대만큼 오르고, 그 위의 움직임만 예측 불가능하다.

Problem 3Information costs and Grossman–Stiglitz

A fund with $1bn under management spends $2m per year on analysts and data. (a) What annual excess return (alpha) is needed to recover the research cost? (b) What if AUM is $3bn? (c) How does this calculation connect to the claim that "markets cannot be perfectly efficient"?

(a) Breakeven alpha = research cost / AUM = 2,000,000 / 1,000,000,000 = 0.002 = 0.20%
(b) 2,000,000 / 3,000,000,000 = 0.067% — a bigger fund recovers its research cost even from smaller mispricing
(c) If prices already reflect everything and alpha is 0, no one spends $2m → information gathering stops and prices stop reflecting information. So in equilibrium enough mispricing must remain to pay for research (Grossman–Stiglitz).
Key The question is not "Is it efficient?" but "Is the remaining mispricing larger than the cost of finding it?"

문제 3정보비용과 Grossman–Stiglitz

운용자산 $1bn 인 펀드가 애널리스트·데이터에 연 $2m 을 쓴다. (a) 연구비를 회수하려면 연 몇 %의 초과수익(알파)이 필요한가? (b) 운용자산이 $3bn 이면? (c) 이 계산이 "시장은 완전히 효율적일 수 없다"는 주장과 어떻게 연결되나?

(a) 손익분기 알파 = 연구비 / AUM = 2,000,000 / 1,000,000,000 = 0.002 = 0.20%
(b) 2,000,000 / 3,000,000,000 = 0.067% — 큰 펀드는 더 작은 mispricing 으로도 연구비를 회수한다
(c) 가격이 이미 모든 것을 반영해 알파가 0 이면 아무도 $2m 을 쓰지 않는다 → 정보 수집이 멈추고 가격은 정보를 반영하지 못한다. 그러므로 균형에서는 연구비를 보상할 만큼의 mispricing 이 남아 있어야 한다 (Grossman–Stiglitz).
핵심 물어야 할 것은 "효율적인가?"가 아니라 "남은 mispricing 이 찾는 비용보다 큰가?"이다.

Problem 4Self-destruction of technical analysis

Chart analysts found that Hanbit Chemical bounced every time it fell to $30. (a) What happens once this pattern is widely known? (b) Why is this objection valid even without evidence? (c) Another analyst says, "This stock's chart looks like a random walk, so the market is efficient." Is that right?

(a) Competition to buy first: buy at $31, then $32, $33 … → support is pushed upward and $30 never returns. The rule disappears in the hands of those who found it.
(b) Past prices are free public information, so competition erases them first. If a rule works, trading on it moves prices and removes it — an objection that holds by logic alone (weak form).
(c) No. Shape alone settles nothing. A random walk is a consequence of efficiency, not proof of it.
Key A profitable technical rule disappears on its own because of the people who find it.

문제 4기술적 분석의 자기파괴

차트 분석가들은 한빛화학이 $30 까지 떨어질 때마다 반등했다는 사실을 발견했다. (a) 이 패턴이 널리 알려지면 어떻게 되나? (b) 이것이 증거 없이도 성립하는 반론인 이유는? (c) 다른 분석가는 "이 주식 차트가 random walk 처럼 생겼으니 시장은 효율적"이라고 한다. 옳은가?

(a) 먼저 사려는 경쟁: $31 에서 사고, 다음엔 $32, $33 … → 지지선이 위로 밀려 $30 은 다시 오지 않는다. 규칙이 발견한 사람들 손에 사라진다.
(b) 과거 가격은 공짜 공개정보라 경쟁이 가장 먼저 지운다. 규칙이 통하면 그 규칙을 쓰는 매매가 가격을 움직여 규칙을 없앤다 — 논리만으로 성립하는 반론(weak form).
(c) 아니다. 모양만으로는 아무것도 정해지지 않는다. random walk 는 효율성의 결과이지 증거가 아니다.
핵심 수익을 내는 기술적 규칙은 그것을 찾은 사람들 때문에 스스로 사라진다.

Problem 5Good company vs good investment

Market consensus (already in the current price): Firm A earnings growth 15%, Firm B 3%. Analyst's view: Firm A has excellent management and products but growth of 13%; Firm B is an ordinary company but growth of 6%. If the market is semistrong efficient and the analysis is correct, which stock should you buy?

Step 1 The price already contains the consensus (15%, 3%). Profit comes only from the "difference from market expectations."
Step 2 A: 13% − 15% = −2 pp → worse than market expectations → a good company but no reason to buy (if anything, overvalued)
Step 3 B: 6% − 3% = +3 pp → better than market expectations → buy candidate
Condition To profit anyway, you need better data or interpretation than others consistently, after costs. Such an edge is more plausible in stocks with few analysts.
Key Not "Is it a good company?" but only "Is it better than the market believes?" makes money.

문제 5좋은 기업 vs 좋은 투자

시장 컨센서스(현재 가격에 반영됨): A사 이익성장률 15%, B사 3%. 애널리스트 분석: A사는 경영진·제품 모두 훌륭하지만 성장률 13%, B사는 평범한 기업이지만 성장률 6%. 시장이 semistrong 효율적이고 분석이 옳다면 어느 주식을 사야 하나?

Step 1 가격은 이미 컨센서스(15%, 3%)를 담고 있다. 수익은 "시장 기대와의 차이"에서만 나온다.
Step 2 A: 13% − 15% = −2%p → 시장 기대보다 나쁘다 → 좋은 기업이지만 매수 근거 아님(오히려 고평가 쪽)
Step 3 B: 6% − 3% = +3%p → 시장 기대보다 낫다 → 매수 후보
조건 그래도 이익이 나려면 남보다 나은 자료·해석이 꾸준히, 비용 차감 후 있어야 한다. 그런 우위는 분석가가 적은 종목에서 더 그럴듯하다.
핵심 "좋은 기업인가?"가 아니라 "시장이 믿는 것보다 나은가?"만 돈이 된다.

Problem 6Net return after fees

An active fund has a pre-cost return of 10.4% and its benchmark index returns 9.8%. Management fee 0.95%, trading costs 0.25%. An index fund tracking the same index charges 0.04%. (a) Pre-cost excess return (b) Annual difference between the active investor and the index-fund investor (c) What is the difference in final wealth from investing $100,000 for 20 years? (assume the same return every year)

(a) 10.4 − 9.8 = +0.60% — the research itself succeeded
(b) Active net = 10.4 − 0.95 − 0.25 = 9.20% · Index = 9.8 − 0.04 = 9.76% → difference −0.56 pp

Problem 7The arithmetic of active management

Index funds hold 40% of the whole market and active managers hold 60%. This year's market return is 7.5%. A "star group" of active managers holding 20% of the market earned 9.0% before costs. (a) What is the active average before costs? (b) What did the remaining active managers (40% of the market) earn before costs? (c) If all active costs are 1.1%, what is each group's return after costs?

(a) 0.40(7.5) + 0.60·r̄A = 7.5 → r̄A = 7.5% (if indexers earn the market return, the active average is also the market return)
(b) 0.40(7.5) + 0.20(9.0) + 0.40·rY = 7.5 → 3.0 + 1.8 + 0.40·rY = 7.5 → rY = 2.7 / 0.40 = 6.75%
(c) Star 9.0 − 1.1 = 7.9% · rest 6.75 − 1.1 = 5.65% · active average 7.5 − 1.1 = 6.4% < 7.5%
Key Before costs it is zero-sum: one active manager's excess return is another's loss. After costs, active managers as a group must lose; the only question is whether a "particular manager" can win.

문제 7액티브 운용의 산술

시장 전체의 40% 는 인덱스펀드, 60% 는 액티브 매니저가 보유한다. 올해 시장수익률 7.5%. 액티브 매니저 중 시장의 20% 를 보유한 "스타 그룹"이 비용 전 9.0% 를 벌었다. (a) 액티브 전체의 비용 전 평균은? (b) 나머지 액티브(시장의 40%)는 비용 전 몇 %? (c) 액티브 비용이 모두 1.1% 라면 각 그룹의 비용 후 수익률은?

(a) 0.40(7.5) + 0.60·r̄A = 7.5 → r̄A = 7.5% (인덱스가 시장수익을 벌면 액티브 평균도 시장수익)
(b) 0.40(7.5) + 0.20(9.0) + 0.40·rY = 7.5 → 3.0 + 1.8 + 0.40·rY = 7.5 → rY = 2.7 / 0.40 = 6.75%
(c) 스타 9.0 − 1.1 = 7.9% · 나머지 6.75 − 1.1 = 5.65% · 액티브 평균 7.5 − 1.1 = 6.4% < 7.5%
핵심 비용 전에는 제로섬: 누군가의 초과수익은 다른 액티브의 손실. 비용 후 액티브 전체는 질 수밖에 없고, 문제는 "특정 매니저"가 이길 수 있느냐뿐.

Problem 8Portfolio management in an efficient market

A 32-year-old semiconductor engineer. High income tax bracket, plans to buy a home in 5 years, and holds 70% of financial assets in her own company and other semiconductor stocks. She says, "If the market is efficient, any stock is the same." What is wrong, and what should she do?

What is wrong Efficiency is a claim about prices (the market cannot tell you which stock is cheap), not that every portfolio is the same for every person.
Hedging and diversification Her pay and her stocks both depend on semiconductors → reduce this exposure and diversify widely (avoidable risk earns no reward)
Taxes High tax rate → choose tax-efficient assets and accounts (she wants different securities than a tax-exempt pension fund)
Horizon and liquidity Home down payment in 5 years → hold that part in low-volatility, liquid assets. Set the stock/safe-asset mix to her risk tolerance.
Key Even in an efficient market there is work to do — just not picking stocks.

Problem 9Matching the three hurdles

Match each situation to one of the three testing hurdles (magnitude / selection bias / lucky event). (a) Testing 200 trading rules published in books and papers shows no excess return after costs → "no rule makes money" (b) A $50bn pension fund manager earns 0.05% more per year through skill (c) A fund-rating firm presents the top fund of 5,000 over the past 8 years as a "genius manager"

(a) Selection bias — someone with a truly profitable rule does not publish it. Published rules are those not good enough to keep secret.
(b) Magnitude — 50,000,000,000 × 0.0005 = $25m/year. Real value, but statistically invisible in return noise.
(c) Lucky event — a remarkable record picked in advance is striking; picked after the fact from thousands, it is ordinary.
Key None of the three can be fixed with better data — a reason the EMH is hard to refute, not a reason to believe it.

문제 9세 장애물 짝짓기

(a) 선택편향 (selection bias) — 정말 돈 버는 규칙을 가진 사람은 공개하지 않는다. 공개된 규칙은 비밀로 할 만큼 좋지 않았던 것들이다.
(b) 크기 (magnitude) — 50,000,000,000 × 0.0005 = $25m/년. 진짜 가치지만 수익률 잡음 속에서 통계적으로 안 보인다.
(c) 운 (lucky event) — 미리 골랐다면 놀라운 기록도 수천 개 중 사후에 고르면 평범하다.
핵심 셋 다 더 좋은 데이터로 고칠 수 없다 — EMH 를 반박하기 어려운 이유이지, 믿을 이유는 아니다.

Problem 11Joint hypothesis and measurement window

Researchers compute the normal return of a group of stocks but cannot agree on whether β is 0.7 or 1.3. The market risk premium is 6% per year, and 1 year = 252 trading days. (a) How large is the difference in normal return the two β values produce per year, cumulated over 3 years, and per day? (b) If you find a 3-year holding "abnormal return" of 10%, what can you conclude? (c) Why is a short-window event study the cleanest evidence?

(a) Annual (1.3 − 0.7) × 6% = 3.6% · 3 years ≈ 3 × 3.6 = 10.8% · daily 3.6 / 252 = 0.014%
(b) Almost nothing. The 10% can appear or vanish from model choice alone (10.8% gap) → cannot tell whether the market is inefficient or the model missed risk = the joint-hypothesis problem
(c) In a one- or two-day window the model difference is about 0.014%, negligible next to a price reaction of several percent → the conclusion is the same under any model.
Key An efficiency test is always a joint test of "efficiency + pricing model." The shorter the window, the smaller the model's influence.

문제 11결합가설과 측정 기간

연구자들이 어떤 주식 집단의 정상수익을 계산하는데, β 를 0.7 로 볼지 1.3 으로 볼지 합의하지 못한다. 시장위험프리미엄은 연 6%, 1년 = 252 거래일. (a) 두 β 가 만드는 정상수익 차이는 연간, 3년 누적, 하루 기준으로 각각 얼마인가? (b) 3년 보유 "비정상수익" 10% 를 발견했다면 무엇을 결론 내릴 수 있나? (c) 왜 단기 event study 가 가장 깨끗한 증거인가?

(a) 연간 (1.3 − 0.7) × 6% = 3.6% · 3년 ≈ 3 × 3.6 = 10.8% · 하루 3.6 / 252 = 0.014%
(b) 거의 아무것도. 10% 는 모형 선택(10.8% 차이)만으로 생기거나 사라질 수 있다 → 비효율인지, 모형이 위험을 놓쳤는지 구분 불가 = 결합가설 문제
(c) 하루이틀 창에서는 모형 차이가 0.014% 수준이라 몇 % 의 가격 반응에 비해 무시할 만하다 → 어떤 모형을 써도 결론이 같다.
핵심 효율성 검정은 늘 "효율성 + 가격결정모형"의 결합 검정이다. 창이 짧을수록 모형의 영향이 작다.

Problem 12One-day abnormal return (market model · CAPM)

On the day Daeju Pharma announced a new-drug approval, its stock returned +3.1% while the market returned −1.2%. Market model estimated before the event: a = 0.05%, b = 1.4. Daily risk-free rate 0.01%. (a) AR using the market model (b) AR using CAPM (β = 1.4) (c) Why does it matter that the market fell that day?

(a) Normal = 0.05 + 1.4(−1.2) = 0.05 − 1.68 = −1.63% → e = 3.1 − (−1.63) = +4.73%
(b) Normal = 0.01 + 1.4(−1.2 − 0.01) = 0.01 − 1.694 = −1.684% → e = 3.1 + 1.684 = +4.784%
Compare The two models differ by only 4.784 − 4.73 = 0.054 pp — in a one-day window the choice of model does not change the conclusion
(c) A stock with β = 1.4 should have fallen about 1.6% on a day the market fell 1.2%. So the actual +3.1% understates the event effect, which is larger than 3.1%. Looking only at raw returns underestimates the reaction.
Key AR = actual − "the return it would have earned anyway from that day's market move."

(c) β = 1.4 인 주식은 시장이 1.2% 빠진 날 원래 약 1.6% 빠졌어야 한다. 그래서 실제 +3.1% 의 사건 효과는 3.1% 보다 크다. 원수익률만 보면 반응을 과소평가한다.

Problem 15Momentum, reversals, and transaction costs

(c) Does this result prove inefficiency?

(a) A: short-run momentum → market underreaction · B: long-run reversal → overreaction. Both use only past returns, so they test the weak form.
(b) Cost = 0.60 × 1.2% = 0.72%/month → net excess = 1.0 − 0.72 = 0.28%/month (illustrative numbers). 72% of the paper profit disappears in trading costs.

Problem 16Three interpretations of an anomaly

Choose which of the three interpretations of an anomaly (risk premium / inefficiency / data mining) each claim is. (a) "High B/M firms are distressed, so their high returns compensate for default risk" (b) "Investors incorporate information about neglected stocks late, so patient investors can exploit the gap" (c) "The result of running hundreds of variables on the same decades of US data" (d) What evidence weakens (c)? (e) Explain, from an efficient-market view, the result that market returns were high when the dividend yield was high.

(a) Risk premium — the return is real but not abnormal. The benchmark model is incomplete.
(b) Inefficiency — prices do not reflect information in time, a truly abnormal return · (c) Data mining — an artifact of searching, never there
(d) Does it survive out of sample: other countries, later periods, data the original authors did not see
(e) Required return rises when the economy is bad and risk is hard to bear = time-varying risk premium. The same data also fit the inefficiency reading, so the data cannot distinguish them.
Key The issue for every anomaly: compensation for omitted risk, free money the market left behind, or chance?

문제 16Anomaly 의 세 해석

각 주장이 anomaly 의 세 해석(위험프리미엄 / 비효율 / 데이터 마이닝) 중 무엇인지 고르라. (a) "고 B/M 기업은 곤경에 빠진 기업이라 높은 수익은 부도위험의 보상" (b) "투자자들은 소외 종목의 정보를 늦게 반영해 인내심 있는 투자자가 그 틈을 이용할 수 있다" (c) "같은 미국 데이터 수십 년에 수백 개 변수를 돌린 결과" (d) (c) 를 약화시키는 증거는? (e) 배당수익률이 높을 때 시장수익이 높았다는 결과를 효율적 시장 관점에서 설명하라.

(a) 위험프리미엄 — 수익은 진짜지만 비정상은 아니다. 벤치마크 모형이 불완전.
(b) 비효율 — 가격이 정보를 제때 반영 못 함, 진짜 비정상수익 · (c) 데이터 마이닝 — 탐색의 산물, 애초에 없었다
(d) 표본 밖(out of sample)에서도 살아남는가: 다른 나라, 이후 기간, 원저자가 보지 못한 데이터
(e) 경기가 나빠 위험을 지기 어려울 때 요구수익률이 높아진다 = 시간가변 위험프리미엄. 같은 데이터가 비효율 해석과도 맞아 데이터로는 구분 불가.
핵심 모든 anomaly 의 쟁점: 누락된 위험의 보상인가, 시장이 남긴 공짜 돈인가, 우연인가.

Problem 17PEAD long-short strategy

For stocks in the top 10% of earnings surprise, CAR moved from 2.6% the day after the announcement to 5.4% 60 days later; for the bottom 10%, from −2.1% to −4.5%. You buy the top and short the bottom the day after the announcement and hold 60 days. (a) What is the long-short abnormal return before costs? (b) What if the round-trip transaction cost is 1.0% per quarter? (c) Why is this anomaly especially awkward for semistrong efficiency?

(a) Long: 5.4 − 2.6 = +2.8 pp · Short: −(−4.5 − (−2.1)) = +2.4 pp → total 5.2 pp (60 days)
(b) 5.2 − 1.0 = 4.2 pp — still positive after costs (on the assumed numbers)
(c) ① Earnings are public on the announcement day — no private information needed ② the drift is slow, over weeks ③ if it were risk, risk would have to change exactly at the announcement, in proportion to the size of the surprise — unconvincing.
Key The day 0 jump is what efficiency predicts, but the same-direction drift afterward is underreaction to public information.

문제 17PEAD 롱숏 전략

실적 서프라이즈 상위 10% 종목의 CAR 은 발표 다음 날 2.6% 에서 60일 뒤 5.4% 로, 하위 10% 는 −2.1% 에서 −4.5% 로 움직였다. 발표 다음 날 상위를 사고 하위를 공매도해 60일 보유한다. (a) 비용 전 롱숏 비정상수익은? (b) 왕복 거래비용이 분기당 1.0% 이면? (c) 이 anomaly 가 semistrong 효율성에 특히 곤란한 이유는?

(a) 롱: 5.4 − 2.6 = +2.8%p · 숏: −(−4.5 − (−2.1)) = +2.4%p → 합계 5.2%p (60일)
(b) 5.2 − 1.0 = 4.2%p — 비용 후에도 남는다(가정한 숫자 기준)
(c) ① 실적은 발표일에 공개 — 사적 정보가 필요 없다 ② drift 가 수주에 걸쳐 느리다 ③ 위험 설명이라면 위험이 정확히 발표 시점에, 서프라이즈 크기에 비례해 바뀌어야 한다 — 설득력이 약하다.
핵심 day 0 점프는 효율성의 예측대로 있지만, 그 뒤 같은 방향 drift 는 공개정보에 대한 과소반응이다.

Problem 18Performance persistence and SPIVA

200 funds were split into top 100 / bottom 100 by period-1 performance. In period 2, 54 of the top 100 were top again and 62 of the bottom 100 were bottom again. (a) How many would you expect of each if it were pure luck? (b) Interpret this asymmetry. (c) If 84% of 500 large-cap funds underperformed the index, how many outperformed? Why do published averages look better than reality?

(a) If luck, period-2 rank is unrelated to period 1 → each 100 × 0.5 = 50
(b) Winners repeating 54 (+4 above expected, weak) · losers repeating 62 (+12, clear) → poor performance repeats because of stable high costs, while good performance is mostly luck and rarely repeats.
(c) 500 × (1 − 0.84) = 80. Survivorship bias: funds that closed for poor results drop out of the sample, so the surviving average flatters the industry.
Key This record arises even if the EMH is not exactly true — even small inefficiencies mostly vanish after costs.

문제 18성과 지속성과 SPIVA

펀드 200개를 1기 성과로 상위 100 / 하위 100 으로 나누었다. 2기에 상위 100 중 54개가 다시 상위, 하위 100 중 62개가 다시 하위였다. (a) 순전히 운이라면 각각 몇 개를 기대하나? (b) 이 비대칭을 해석하라. (c) 500개 대형주 펀드 중 84% 가 지수를 하회했다면 상회한 펀드 수는? 공개 평균이 실제보다 좋아 보이는 이유는?

(a) 운이면 2기 순위가 1기와 무관 → 각 100 × 0.5 = 50개
(b) 승자 반복 54 (기대보다 +4, 약함) · 패자 반복 62 (+12, 뚜렷) → 나쁜 성과는 안정적인 높은 비용 때문에 반복되고, 좋은 성과는 대체로 운이라 잘 반복되지 않는다.
(c) 500 × (1 − 0.84) = 80개. 생존편향: 부진해서 문 닫은 펀드가 표본에서 빠져 살아남은 펀드 평균이 업계를 미화한다.
핵심 이 기록은 EMH 가 정확히 참이 아니어도 나온다 — 작은 비효율도 비용을 빼면 대부분 사라진다.
